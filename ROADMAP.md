# Roadmap

What comes next for Blackjack Rover. This is the one place where the plan is kept; the rest of the repository describes the design.

1. **Adapter plate BJ-P01**, on the existing holes of the UGV02 deck.
2. **Drawings and flat patterns** of the three sheet-metal parts, with the bend data of the shop that makes them.
3. **First set of parts**: cut, bent, fitted on the chassis.
4. **Power on the bench**: the measurements listed in section 7 of the [power supply study](studies/BJ-STU-001_power_supply_study.md).
5. **Bill of materials and cable list.**
6. **ROS 2 bring-up** on the Arduino VENTUNO Q: drive, odometry, depth camera, navigation.
7. **Motor control on the STM32**: study on moving it to the real-time side of the board.
8. **Options**: lidar, pan-tilt turret, GNSS RTK, cellular modem.
9. **The decision record**, published alongside each step.
