Air around the board, not chip temperature. This steady-state model cannot predict the board, SoM or junction temperature, local hot spots or time to heat up. Arduino rates VENTUNO Q for −10 to 60 °C ambient air. The 50 °C caution boundary is a chosen 10 °C engineering margin, not an Arduino throttling threshold. Above 60 °C is outside the published range, not a predicted shutdown. The complete rover and battery still need validation.

Open the model & assumptions

Based on BJ-STU-002 revision C. The fan operating point intersects the lower estimated fan curve with grille and inlet losses. Heat balance: ΔT = heat / (air density × heat capacity × airflow). Fan mode takes no credit for heat escaping through the walls. Passive mode solves buoyancy flow and, in shade, wall heat loss. Air properties remain fixed at 30 °C.

Sun means 900 W/m², a black cover, no wind and 12 W inward heat. Fan off means fan absent, not an obstructing stopped rotor. Slots without a fan extend the study’s buoyancy model to the larger outlet area; that combination was not one of the three original study cases. Slider extremes explore the model, not qualified hardware conditions.
