# Cover rail mounting detail

`blackjack-cover-rail-cutaway.png` is a sectioned render of the original Fusion assembly export used for the reveal. Source: cover v0.29 (`o155_b000`), +Y UGV02 rail (`o056_b000`), first +Y M3 T-nut and screw (`o153_b000`, `o153_b001`). The export retains world coordinates in metres. The rail/flange fixing is retained in the v0.32 design; its fabrication script and trial sheet describe M3 screws, internal T-nuts and side-wall tool access.

The presentation retains a short rail and cover section, x = −73 to −60 mm, y = 84 to 125 mm, z = −1 to 25 mm. The complete screw and nut remain visible at their assembled positions. The clipping exposes their relationship without changing the source assembly. Colours identify parts and do not specify finishes. Hardware is the simplified CAD representation, including omitted threads. No physical assembly or fit validation is claimed.

Numbered HTML/SVG callouts identify the screw, cover flange, internal nut and existing rail; the text legend remains readable on mobile. The full-size PNG opens separately. Image rights follow the existing Blackjack rendering terms.
